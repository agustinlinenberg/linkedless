What the labels mean

LinkedLess classifies posts by pattern and shows you which pattern matched. It quotes the post's own words back to you and never writes a claim about a person or guesses at anyone's motives.

A label describes a kind of post, not the person who wrote it, and it will be wrong sometimes. Read the original before you conclude anything.

Posts about death, illness, job loss and disaster go down a separate path that states the facts and stops. If one of those ever gets a snarky label, that is a bug. Please report it.
